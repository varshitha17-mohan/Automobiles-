ABOUT THE BRAKE SYSTEM

The braking system helps slow down or stop a vehicle. It is essential for safe driving and controlling the vehicle in different traffic conditions.

04 / VEHICLE SAFETY SYSTEM

STOP SAFELY. DRIVE CONFIDENTLY.

When the driver presses the brake pedal, the braking system applies force to help reduce the vehicle's speed. Modern vehicles may use disc brakes, drum brakes or a combination of both.

KEY FUNCTIONS

  • Helps slow down and stop the vehicle.
  • Supports vehicle control during braking.
  • Converts motion energy into heat through friction.
  • Works with safety systems such as ABS in equipped vehicles.

TYPES OF BRAKES

🔴

DISC BRAKES

Use brake pads that press against a rotating disc to slow down the wheel.

⚙

DRUM BRAKES

Use brake shoes that press against the inside of a rotating drum to create friction.

🛡

ABS

Anti-lock Braking System helps prevent the wheels from locking during hard braking.
