ABOUT THE ENGINE

The engine is one of the most important components of a vehicle. It converts energy into mechanical power to move the vehicle.

01 / ENGINE SYSTEM

POWERING YOUR JOURNEY

A vehicle engine generates the power needed to move the wheels. It works together with other systems, including the transmission, cooling system and fuel system.

KEY FUNCTIONS

  • Produces mechanical power.
  • Works with the transmission system.
  • Supports vehicle movement and performance.
  • Operates with cooling and lubrication systems.

TYPES OF ENGINES

⚙

PETROL ENGINE

Uses petrol and spark ignition to produce power. Commonly used in cars and motorcycles.

🔧

DIESEL ENGINE

Uses compression ignition and is widely used in many cars, trucks and commercial vehicles.

⚡

ELECTRIC MOTOR

Converts electrical energy into mechanical motion in electric vehicles.
