ABOUT THE TYRE

Tyres connect your vehicle to the road. They provide grip, support the vehicle's weight and help deliver a comfortable driving experience.

02 / TYRE SYSTEM

YOUR CONNECTION TO THE ROAD

Tyres are essential for safe driving. Their tread patterns help provide traction on different road surfaces, while their construction supports the vehicle and absorbs some road irregularities.

KEY FUNCTIONS

  • Provides grip between the road and vehicle.
  • Supports the vehicle's weight.
  • Helps with steering and braking.
  • Contributes to driving comfort and stability.
